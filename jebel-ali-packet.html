Analysis Moderate-confidence evidence of flooding at Jebel Ali after the 16 April 2024 storm. Rainfall peaked at 53.39 mm on 16 April, above the 50 mm/day level that gives full rainfall support. Sentinel-1 radar, which is not affected by cloud, shows 2.7% of the area darkening between 12 and 24 April, the typical signal of standing water. Landsat 9 flags 4.05% and Sentinel-2 0.85%. After removing permanent water such as the sea and port basins, 0.74% of land pixels are flagged by all three sensors. Nine official and public reports support flooding in the area. The sensors disagree on extent because they image on different days: Sentinel-2 and Landsat 9 see the area 3–6 days after the rain, when much water had drained, and radar responds to wet soil as well as open water. The high-agreement area is therefore the most reliable estimate.
